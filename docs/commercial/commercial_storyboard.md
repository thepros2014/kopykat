# KopyKat Commercial Production Kit: "The Twenty Dollar Numskull"

**Format:** 60-Second 3D Animated Commercial  
**Style:** Pixar/Illumination 3D Animation | B2B Slapstick Satire  
**Core Value Proposition:** Raw AI only outputs text. KopyKat orchestrates your entire e-commerce operation in seconds.

### Scene 3: The Bottleneck (0:30 - 0:45)
