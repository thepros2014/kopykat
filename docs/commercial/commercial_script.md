# KopyKat Commercial Script: "The Twenty Dollar Numskull"

**Format:** 60-Second 3D Animated Commercial | **Tone:** B2B Satire & Comedy

### Characters
- **Cat 1 (The Boss):** Tiny tie, stressed, holding a yellow wiffle bat.
- **Cat 2 (The Innovator):** Smug, sipping coffee from a "One-Person Store" mug.
- **Cat 3 (The Copywriter):** Vacant stare, cross-eyed, slowly tapping a key.

---

### SCENE 1: THE ACCUSATION
*(Office door kicks open. Cat 1 storms in and whacks Cat 2 on the head with the wiffle bat. THWACK!)*

**CAT 1:** "What are you thinkin', numskull?! Basic AI is twenty bucks! Why are you paying for KopyKat?!"

*(Cat 2 rubs his ears, sips his coffee, and grabs the invoice.)*

**CAT 2:** "Gimme that. Look, Einstein. Basic AI just gives you raw text. You still gotta manually paste it, format it, optimize it, and push it everywhere. KopyKat does the whole operation."

---

### SCENE 2: THE DEMO
*(Cat 2 taps a tablet. Camera zooms into the sleek KopyKat dashboard. A single product CSV uploads. In a flash, titles, bullet points, SEO blogs, and ad hooks are generated and syndicated across Shopify, Amazon, eBay, and Mailchimp.)*

**CAT 2:** "One click. It mines competitor one-star reviews, writes counter-copy, generates image campaigns, and syncs every sales channel. Now look over there."

---

### SCENE 3: THE BOTTLENECK
*(Camera whip-pans to Cat 3 sitting in the corner. A blinking cursor sits on a blank screen. A fly buzzes around his head. He taps the spacebar once.)*

**CAT 2:** "You pay that guy forty grand a year for what? It has been five weeks and he has not finished one catalog description!"

---

### SCENE 4: VICTORY
*(Camera pans back to Cat 2. The tablet chimes with a green badge: "Omni-Campaign Synchronized".)*

**CAT 2:** "Done. Entire catalog live in ten seconds."

*(Cat 2 takes the bat from Cat 1. THWACK! He taps Cat 1 on the head, leans back, and smirks.)*

**CAT 2:** "Victory."
