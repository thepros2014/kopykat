Do I need any coding or technical skills to use KopyKat? +
No coding is required. Start with a product detail, choose a workflow, and review the result in the dashboard. Connector setup depends on the platform and the permissions it requires.
Will using AI copy get my store banned on Amazon or Etsy? +
KopyKat is designed to help you create platform-aware drafts, but you remain responsible for reviewing content and following each marketplace's current policies before publishing.
How does the inventory stock sync work? +
Connected stores can share inventory updates through the available integration workflows. Exact timing and supported actions depend on the platform connection, permissions, and your plan.

Plans are managed from the dashboard. Review the billing terms shown at checkout for your selected plan, and contact support if you need help changing your subscription.
