# 🚀 SnapCopy AI: Month 1 Playbook

This is your exact blueprint to get your first paying customers. The tech is fully automated—your only job now is distribution.

## Week 1: The "Hustle" Phase (Get 50 Free Signups)

Your goal isn't to make money yet. It's to get 50 people to sign up for the free tier. Let the automated email drip campaigns convert them to paid.

**Daily Tasks:**
*   **Facebook Groups:** Post in 1 new group daily (*Shopify Entrepreneurs, Amazon FBA, Dropshipping*).
    *   *Script:* "Got tired of spending hours writing product descriptions so I built a tool that does it instantly. Free to try (no credit card). Let me know if the copy is actually good! 👉 https://snapcopy-ai.onrender.com"
*   **Reddit Scout:** Check your email for leads generated by the Opportunity Scout. Copy/paste the AI-drafted replies on Reddit.
*   **Twitter/X:** Post 1 "Before & After" copy example. Show a boring product description, then show the SnapCopy AI version. Include the link.

**One-Time Tasks:**
*   Post on `r/SideProject` and `r/entrepreneur`.
*   Submit the site to free AI directories (e.g., *There's An AI For That*, *Futurepedia*).

---

## Week 2: The "Launch" Phase

Now that the site has some initial traffic and the database is warmed up, go for a bigger audience.

**Tuesday (12:01 AM PST): Product Hunt Launch**
*   Create a clean, catchy tagline: *"SnapCopy AI - The 2-second copywriter for founders."*
*   Take 3-4 good screenshots of the dashboard and the live code API on your homepage.
*   Post a "Maker Comment" introducing yourself, explaining why you built it, and highlighting the free tier.

**Rest of the Week:**
*   **Cold Email/DM:** Find 10 small digital marketing agencies on LinkedIn. Message them: *"Hey [Name], built an API that automates ad copy generation. Got a free tier if your team wants to play around with it: [Link]"*
*   Check your Stripe dashboard. The Week 1 free signups are now receiving their "Day 7: Tokens expiring" automated emails. This is when your first $9/mo upgrades should happen.

---

## Week 3: The "Content & Developer" Phase

Start targeting developers and technical founders who want to integrate your API.

*   **HackerNews (Show HN):** Post "Show HN: SnapCopy AI – Generate marketing copy via API". Technical crowds love the direct API access you built.
*   **RapidAPI:** Create a free developer account on RapidAPI and list your API. Millions of developers search there for tools just like this.
*   **Check the Blog:** Your SEO engine has been publishing 3 posts a week. Go to `https://snapcopy-ai.onrender.com/blog`. Take the best-looking article and share it on LinkedIn or Twitter as a value-add post.

---

## Week 4: The "Optimize" Phase

Evaluate what worked and double down.

*   **Analyze Data:** Look at your Stripe Dashboard and your local database (`/admin/revenue`). Where did your paying customers come from?
*   **Ask for Feedback:** Email your active free users (the ones who used their 5,000 tokens but didn't upgrade). Ask: *"What feature would make this worth $9/month to you?"*
*   **Double Down:** If Facebook groups brought in 80% of your traffic, stop posting on Twitter and spend an hour a day engaging in Facebook comments. If the API users are upgrading to the $29/mo plan, focus entirely on developer forums.

---

## 🛑 The "Do Not Do This" List

1.  **Do not pay for ads (yet).** Don't run Google/Meta ads until you know exactly what messaging converts free users to paid users.
2.  **Do not spam.** If you just drop links on Reddit without context, you will be banned. Always provide value or ask for feedback.
3.  **Do not write blogs manually.** Let the automated SEO engine do its job. Your time is better spent talking to humans in forums and DMs.
